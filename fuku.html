Case Study 03 — Packaging Design

FUKU
Brew

A new bottle label and cap for FUKU, a Japanese-style rice lager brewed in Italy, as the brand tried moving from cans to bottles.

Client
FUKU Brew, Rome
Deliverables
Front & back label, bottle cap
Format
330 ml bottle
Timeline
Short project, 2025
01

The Brief

FUKU is a crisp, rice-brewed lager with a Japanese spirit, brewed in Italy and usually sold in cans. The brewery wanted to try bottles, so the brief was to redesign the packaging for a 330 ml bottle: a front label, a back label and a bottle cap.

02

The Label

The label is built around the brand's line, “Japanese inspiration, Italian heart.” A Great Wave-style sea breaks around the Colosseum under a red rising sun, with FUKU brushed down a paper banner in the centre. The red, navy and cream palette carries onto the back label, which holds ingredients in Italian, English and German, plus the brand's “Fuku monk” toast.

03

The Cap

The cap is the first thing you see in a fridge, so it's kept simple: the character 福 (fuku, meaning happiness or good fortune) on the red sun from the label. I tried it in two weights of cream, in white and in navy.

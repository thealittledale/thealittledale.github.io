Case Study 03 – Campaign & Poster

Time's
Ticking

A poster, animated billboard and leaflet for UN Global Goal 7, Affordable and Clean Energy, made for a Global Goals exhibition with Project Everyone.

Brief
The Global Goals, with Project Everyone
Module
TG2BP, Brand & Persuasion 2 (Year 2, 2026)
Tools
Procreate, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects
Deliverables
A2 poster, 48-sheet animation, DL leaflet
01

The Brief

With four years left to reach the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals by 2030, the brief, set with UN partner agency Project Everyone, was to design exhibition material that cuts through the pessimism and gets the Goals back on the agenda. Each student was given one Goal. Mine was Goal 7: Affordable and Clean Energy.

The campaign had to work across an A2 poster, a 12-second animated roadside billboard and a folded leaflet, all with a clear call to action.

02

Concept

I took my lead from Abram Games, Britain's wartime poster artist, and his idea of “maximum meaning, minimum means”: combining two objects into one image so the message reads instantly. I sketched pairs that share a shape but carry opposite meanings, like a light bulb and an oil drop, or a sun and a clock face.

The idea that stuck was a wind turbine whose blades become the hands of a clock: clean energy, and time running out to reach it by 2030.

03

Development

Feedback shaped each version. I made the turbine bolder so it didn't get lost in the clock, set it in rolling hills, and added a line about the 2030 deadline to make the urgency clear. I also tried a looser, hand-painted version, but its busy image hid the clock idea, so I went with the cleaner design.

Type is Tablet Gothic, condensed for the headline and regular width for the body, set in lowercase to state the facts calmly rather than shout. Blues, greens and yellows sit side by side on the colour wheel, with one orange clock hand carrying the urgency. After printing a test, I matched the colours to the department's CMYK swatches, and checked the design in a colour-blindness simulator.

04

Animated Billboard

For the 12-second roadside billboard I kept just the strongest parts: the turbine-clock, the title and the 2030 line. The blades spin as the clock hand ticks round. I storyboarded it in Procreate, built each frame in InDesign so everything sat on a grid, then animated it in After Effects.

05

Leaflet

The DL leaflet carries the same palette, type and centred layout, with the facts and seven practical things people can do, from switching off at the socket to moving to a green energy supplier. In feedback the white clouds competed with the turbine, so I dropped them to 30% opacity to keep the focus on the main image.

06

Reflection

I think the final pieces get the message across clearly, and the three formats feel like one campaign. If I did it again, I'd spend more time on analogue methods like letterpress early on, before moving to digital.

The Global Goals icons and logo belong to the United Nations and were provided as part of the brief.
